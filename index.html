Install

Up and clipping in two minutes.

Three steps, once. After that it just works every time you play.

  1. 1

    Get a script manager

    Install Violentmonkey or Tampermonkey from your browser's store. It's a tiny, trusted extension that runs small helpers like this one.

  2. 2

    Install ClippedNow

    Hit the download button. Your script manager asks for one confirmation - accept it and you're done. Updates arrive on their own.

Questions

Asked often.

Is it really free?

Yes. Download it, use it, keep it updated — all free, no account and no locked features.

Will it slow down my game?

On most PCs you won't feel it. If your game stutters, pick the Low-end PC preset: it drops the recording load while the game stays smooth.

Where do my clips live?

In your browser, on your PC. Open them with F9 any time, download the ones you want to keep, delete the rest.

My clip has no sound. What now?

Make sure Game audio is ticked in the panel and your game isn't muted. If you want your voice too, tick Mic and allow the browser to use your microphone.

Can it record my Discord call?

Not directly — browsers can't hear other apps or tabs. The workaround: route Discord through a free virtual cable like VoiceMeeter, then pick that cable as the mic input in the panel.

Which browsers work?

Chrome, Edge and Firefox, with the Violentmonkey or Tampermonkey extension installed.
